# AI Process Lab — Course Curriculum Outline

## Course Purpose

Guide non-technical team members through discovering, mapping, testing, and iterating on AI-augmented workflows using the AI Process Lab canvas tool. The course prioritizes understanding over convenience — learners build fluency with AI primitives by doing the work themselves.

---

## Cluster 1: Understanding Your Work (Foundation)
**Slug:** `understanding-your-work`  
**Order:** 1  
**Description:** Before you can improve a process, you need to see it clearly. Learn to articulate the invisible steps in your daily work.

### Lessons:
1. **Why Process Mapping Matters** (`why-process-mapping`) — Why making invisible work visible is the first step to improving it
2. **The Anatomy of a Workflow** (`anatomy-of-workflow`) — Breaking any task into discrete, observable steps using the 6-field schema
3. **Identifying Your Deliverables** (`identifying-deliverables`) — Mapping the things you actually produce and the steps that create them
4. **Finding Pain Points and Bottlenecks** (`finding-pain-points`) — Recognizing where time, energy, or quality gets lost in your process

---

## Cluster 2: The AI Primitives (Foundation)
**Slug:** `ai-primitives`  
**Order:** 2  
**Description:** Understand the building blocks of AI tools — models, prompts, projects, and connectors — so you can use them deliberately.

### Lessons:
1. **What Is an AI Model?** (`what-is-a-model`) — Understanding the different models (Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi, Claude, OpenAI) and what each is good at
2. **The Art of the Prompt** (`art-of-prompt`) — How to give clear instructions to AI models and why specificity matters
3. **Projects and Context** (`projects-and-context`) — How AI tools use context windows and memory to produce better outputs
4. **Connectors and Integrations** (`connectors-integrations`) — How AI connects to your existing tools and data sources

---

## Cluster 3: Using the Lab (Foundation)
**Slug:** `using-the-lab`  
**Order:** 3  
**Description:** A hands-on walkthrough of the AI Process Lab interface — boards, nodes, connectors, and how to build your first flow.

### Lessons:
1. **Your First Board** (`first-board`) — Creating a workspace and understanding the canvas interface
2. **Working with Nodes** (`working-with-nodes`) — Sticky notes, process steps, human input nodes, and how to place and connect them
3. **Configuring AI Nodes** (`configuring-ai-nodes`) — Selecting models, writing prompts, and running your first AI-powered step
4. **Reading Your Results** (`reading-results`) — Understanding outputs, token usage, and what the metrics mean
5. **Saving and Sharing** (`saving-sharing`) — Version history, sharing boards for review, and leaving comments

---

## Cluster 4: Mapping Your First Flow (Specialization)
**Slug:** `mapping-first-flow`  
**Order:** 4  
**Description:** Put it all together — map a real deliverable from your work as a complete flow, step by step.

### Lessons:
1. **Choosing Your First Deliverable** (`choosing-deliverable`) — How to pick the right process to map first (not too simple, not too complex)
2. **The Workflow Mapping Method** (`workflow-mapping-method`) — Using the 6-field schema to document each step: description, tools, input, output, time, pain points
3. **Connecting the Steps** (`connecting-steps`) — Building the flow with directional connectors and understanding data flow
4. **Where Does AI Fit?** (`where-ai-fits`) — Identifying which steps could benefit from AI assistance and which shouldn't

---

## Cluster 5: Experimenting with AI (Specialization)
**Slug:** `experimenting-with-ai`  
**Order:** 5  
**Description:** Design and run experiments to test whether AI actually improves specific steps in your workflow.

### Lessons:
1. **Designing an Experiment** (`designing-experiment`) — Framing a clear hypothesis: "AI can improve step X by doing Y"
2. **Choosing the Right Model** (`choosing-model`) — When to use DeepSeek for research, Kimi for synthesis, Claude for writing, and how to decide
3. **Running and Comparing** (`running-comparing`) — Using A/B testing to compare models and prompt variations side by side
4. **Evaluating Outputs** (`evaluating-outputs`) — How to rate results honestly using the 1-5 scale and why your notes matter
5. **Iterating on Prompts** (`iterating-prompts`) — The cycle of test, evaluate, refine — and knowing when to stop

---

## Cluster 6: Measuring What Matters (Specialization)
**Slug:** `measuring-what-matters`  
**Order:** 6  
**Description:** Learn to track the right metrics so your experiments produce actionable insights, not just interesting outputs.

### Lessons:
1. **What to Measure and Why** (`what-to-measure`) — Quality, speed, cost, and reliability — the four dimensions of a useful AI step
2. **Reading Token Usage** (`reading-token-usage`) — Understanding costs per node and per flow, and optimizing for budget
3. **The Decision Log** (`decision-log`) — Why documenting what you tried, what worked, and what didn't is the most valuable output
4. **From Experiment to Insight** (`experiment-to-insight`) — Turning your evaluation data into clear recommendations

---

## Cluster 7: Working with Your Team (Specialization)
**Slug:** `working-with-team`  
**Order:** 7  
**Description:** Your workflows don't exist in isolation. Learn to collaborate, get reviews, and build on each other's discoveries.

### Lessons:
1. **Sharing for Review** (`sharing-for-review`) — How to share a board with your approver or collaborator and what to ask for
2. **Giving Useful Feedback** (`giving-feedback`) — How to leave comments that help someone improve their flow
3. **Building on Others' Work** (`building-on-others`) — Referencing a coworker's flow node when your processes overlap
4. **The Team Knowledge Base** (`team-knowledge-base`) — How individual experiments become collective intelligence over time

---

## Cluster 8: Real-World Scenarios (Specialization)
**Slug:** `real-world-scenarios`  
**Order:** 8  
**Description:** Worked examples showing how different roles can map and test their specific workflows.

### Lessons:
1. **Social Media Content Creation** (`social-media-flow`) — Mapping the journey from brief to published post, with AI at research and drafting stages
2. **Blog Post Production** (`blog-post-flow`) — From topic ideation through drafting, editing, and SEO optimization
3. **PR Announcement Workflow** (`pr-announcement-flow`) — Structuring press releases with AI-assisted research and tone calibration
4. **Design Asset Production** (`design-asset-flow`) — How designers can map their creative process and test AI at ideation and iteration stages
5. **Investor Relations Reporting** (`investor-relations-flow`) — Data synthesis, narrative construction, and quality assurance for stakeholder communications
